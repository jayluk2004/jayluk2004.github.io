Projects

Do Audit Red Flags Reveal Earnings Management and Hard-to-Forecast Earnings?

RWRDSdplyrggplot2regressionR Markdown

Do firms that report a material weakness in internal control, or that restate their financial statements, manage earnings more? And do analysts find their earnings harder to forecast? We combined Audit Analytics, Compustat, CRSP and I/B/E/S data for about 28,000 U.S. firm-years (2016 to 2025), estimated discretionary accruals with the modified Jones model and ran fixed-effects regressions.

Finding: firms with a material weakness have clearly larger discretionary accruals and larger analyst forecast errors. Restated years also show more earnings management, but restatements are not linked to less accurate forecasts.
